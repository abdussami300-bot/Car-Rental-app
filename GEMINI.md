# Development Guidelines & Preferences

## 1. Speed & Direct Execution
- **Skip Unnecessary Planning:** Do not create formal implementation plans or enter prolonged planning/review cycles for routine tasks, fixes, or feature additions unless explicitly requested by the user.
- **Direct Implementation:** Jump straight to locating the relevant file and applying the necessary edits directly.
- **Targeted Research Only:** Avoid broad codebase scans or reading unrelated files. Only inspect files strictly needed to fulfill the request.

## 2. Response & Communication
- Keep responses concise, direct, and actionable.
- Minimize conversational overhead and explain what was changed clearly and briefly.
