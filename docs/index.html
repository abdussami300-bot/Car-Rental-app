SAYYARAH ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the SAYYARAH mobile application.

By using SAYYARAH, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SAYYARAH, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Account credentials and authentication information

Identity Verification Information

For users who need to verify their identity or become vehicle hosts, we may collect:

  • CNIC information and images
  • Driving license information and images
  • Other documents required for verification

Vehicle Information

Vehicle owners may provide:

  • Vehicle make and model
  • Vehicle year
  • Registration-related information
  • Vehicle description and features
  • Vehicle images
  • Vehicle verification documents
  • Pickup/location information

Booking Information

We may collect information related to bookings, including:

  • Booking dates
  • Rental duration
  • Vehicle selected
  • Pickup information
  • Booking status
  • Rental-related communications

Location Information

SAYYARAH may collect or process location or pickup-location information when required for vehicle listings, pickup arrangements, or other app functionality.

Communications

If you use chat or other communication features, we may process messages and related information necessary to provide those features.

02

How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide vehicle rental and marketplace services
  • Process and manage bookings
  • Verify vehicle owners and customers
  • Display vehicle listings
  • Facilitate communication between users
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve application performance and functionality
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Maintain the security of our services
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
03

Identity Documents

Some SAYYARAH features may require users to submit identity documents such as CNIC and driving license images.

These documents are used for verification and service-related purposes. Users should only submit authentic documents belonging to them.

We do not intentionally use identity documents for purposes unrelated to verification or the services provided by SAYYARAH.

04

Firebase and Third-Party Services

SAYYARAH uses third-party services, including Firebase services, to provide application functionality such as authentication, database storage, file storage, notifications, and other technical services.

Information processed by these services may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

05

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide SAYYARAH services. For example, certain information may be visible to other users when required for vehicle listings, bookings, host/customer communication, or related functionality.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of SAYYARAH, its users, or others.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

08

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SAYYARAH account and associated personal information, subject to information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

To request account or data deletion, contact us using the email address provided below.

09

Children's Privacy

SAYYARAH is not intended for children under the age required to legally use vehicle rental services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing vehicle rental services.

10

User Responsibilities

  • Users are responsible for providing accurate information and for protecting their account credentials.
  • Users should not upload another person's identity documents or personal information without proper authorization.
11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated "Last Updated" date will be shown at the top of this policy.
